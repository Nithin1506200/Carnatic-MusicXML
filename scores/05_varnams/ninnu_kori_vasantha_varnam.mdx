## Meaning

The following is an approximate meaning cross-checked against the published
notation source.

**Pallavi:** I long for you (“ninnu kori yunnara”). Keep your affection for me
(“nenarunchi”) and heed or protect me (“nannelukora”).

**Anupallavi:** O deity who reclines upon the great serpent
(“pannagashayanudou”), O Sri Parthasaradhi, the charioteer of Arjuna.

**Charanam:** I cannot endure (“korvalera”) the suffering caused by the
bodiless Cupid (“Soonacharuni baari”).
