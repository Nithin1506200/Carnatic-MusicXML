## Meaning

The following is an approximate meaning based on common published
interpretations of this Purandara Dasa devaranama.

**Pallavi:** O Bhagyada Lakshmi — goddess of fortune — please come
("baaramma"), our mother ("nammamma"). Please come, O Soubhagyada Lakshmi —
goddess who bestows auspiciousness and good fortune.

**Charanam 1:** As you step forward, anklets ("gejje") on your feet jingle
with a sweet sound ("dhwani"). At the time of your worship by the good and
devout ("sajjana, sadhu poojaya vela"), the very essence ("benne" — butter)
hidden in the churned curd ("majjige") reveals itself — so does your grace
manifest to the devoted. The exact English meaning of every phrase in this
Kannada lyric is uncertain; translation is approximate.
