## Meaning

O leader of the Pramatha ganas (“Sree Gananatha”), you are vermilion-colored
(“sindhura varna”), an ocean of compassion (“karuna sagara”), and one who has
the face (“vadana”) of a mighty elephant (“kari”).

You have a prominent belly (“Lambodara”). You hold goddess Lakshmi (“lakumi”)
in your hand (“kara”). O son of Mother Parvathi (“Amba sutha”), you are
praised and worshipped (“vinutha”) by the gods (“amara”).

You are served by the groups of siddhas and charanas
(“Siddha charana gana sevita”). O Siddhi Vinayaka, salutations to you again
and again (“te namo namo”).

You are worshipped before all learning and knowledge
(“sakala vidyadi pujita”). O supreme one (“sarvottama”), salutations to you
again and again.
