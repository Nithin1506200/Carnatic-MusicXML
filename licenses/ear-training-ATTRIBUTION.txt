Use and changes:
We selected the 37 source MP3 notes from concert MIDI 48 through 84 for each
instrument, 74 files total. MP3 sample bytes remain unchanged. We packaged
these files in one gzip-compressed TAR archive. During playback the application
retains the sampled attack, phase-matches the sustain loop within the source's
0.6–1.0 second region, and applies a 20 ms in-memory crossfade. Piano and
saxophone holds use sampled sustain loops rather than unlooped natural decay.
