# 5. Varnams

This section holds tana varnam and pada varnam repertoire and related practice
editions.

## Required structure

Record the varnam type, composer, raga, tala, eduppu, source, and section
order. Label sections such as pallavi, anupallavi, muktayi swaras, charanam,
and ettugada/chittaswara passages exactly as supported by the source.

For practice editions, document intended speeds and whether notation represents
first speed, second speed, or both.
